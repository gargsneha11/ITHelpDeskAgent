# Email Is Not Synchronizing

Document ID: KB-GEN-002
Title: Email Is Not Synchronizing
Category: General > Email
Priority: Medium
Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Last Updated: 2026-09-15

## Symptoms

- New messages do not appear in the email application.
- Sent messages remain stuck.
- Email works in webmail but not in the desktop application.
- The application displays an offline or disconnected status.

## Possible Causes

1. Network connectivity issue.
2. Email application is offline.
3. Account authentication has expired.
4. Local application cache issue.
5. Mail service outage.

## Prerequisites

- User should have access to the organization's approved webmail service.
- Support staff should have access to approved service-status information.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Confirm internet connectivity.
2. Check whether email works through the organization's webmail service.
3. Restart the email application.
4. Check the application's connection status.
5. Re-authenticate if prompted.
6. Send a test email.
7. Check whether other users are affected.

## Resolution

Email synchronizes successfully and new messages are available.

## Escalation

Escalate to Messaging Support if:

- Webmail is also unavailable.
- Multiple users are affected.
- Authentication repeatedly fails.
- The issue persists after restarting and re-authentication.

## Security Notes

- Do not request the user's email password.
- Do not ask the user to forward confidential email content unnecessarily.
