# Account Locked

Document ID: KB-ACC-001
Title: Account Locked
Category: Accounts > Account Access
Priority: Medium
Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Owner: IT Service Desk
Last Updated: 2026-09-15

## Symptoms

- User cannot sign in.
- Error indicates that the account is locked or temporarily blocked.
- Multiple unsuccessful login attempts may have occurred.

## Possible Causes

1. Too many incorrect password attempts.
2. An old password is saved on another device.
3. A background application is repeatedly attempting authentication.

## Prerequisites

- User identity must be verified according to organizational policy.
- IT support must have access to the approved account management system.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Confirm the user is entering the correct username.
2. Ask the user to stop repeated login attempts.
3. Check the account status using the approved account management system.
4. If the account is locked, follow the organization's account-unlock procedure.
5. Ask the user to sign in again after the account is unlocked.

## Resolution

The account becomes accessible after the account lock is cleared.

## Escalation

Escalate to Identity and Access Support if:

- The account immediately becomes locked again.
- The account cannot be unlocked.
- Suspicious login activity is detected.

## Security Notes

- Never ask the user to provide their password.
- Never record passwords in support tickets.
